How these are worked out

For each stat, take the higher of the two drivers. Call those six values best.

  • Coverage: sum(best) / 60 × 100
  • Balance: 100 × (1 − stdev(best) / 3), using the population standard deviation, never below 0
  • Overlap: sum(|A − B|) / 60 × 100
  • Synergy: 0.845 × (balance + overlap) − 10.7, kept between 0 and 100

Coverage, balance and overlap match every test the game has shown. The synergy line fits five results, but two of them sit at 0 and 100, so it is not confirmed. It gives coverage no weight; that is unproven. Real drivers may show overlap about one point higher than this, probably because the game rounds the stats it displays. Results are rounded to whole numbers like the game.
